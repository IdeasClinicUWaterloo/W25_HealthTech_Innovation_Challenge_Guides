# Promoting Physical Activity

## Case Study

## Background

Healthcare professionals at Grand River Hospital have noticed that many long-term hospitalized senior patients, aged **65–85**, experience low levels of physical activity.

These patients may be:

- Recovering from surgery
- Managing chronic illnesses
- Undergoing long-term treatments

Due to physical limitations, many patients feel discouraged from exercising.

For some patients, especially those with limited experience using smartphones and other smart devices, television has become a primary source of entertainment. However, television access is not always available in every hospital ward.

As a result, many patients spend extended periods of time in bed, which can contribute to:

- Boredom
- Reduced physical activity
- Increased risk of hospital-induced delirium

---

## Current Challenge

Healthcare professionals recognize that immobility is a significant contributing factor to hospital-induced delirium.

The hospital is seeking technology-driven solutions that encourage patients to participate in engaging physical activities with different levels of difficulty.

A potential solution should provide:

- Physical stimulation
- Mental engagement
- Opportunities for patients to remain active during hospitalization

However, the hospital currently faces challenges including:

- Low patient engagement
- Difficulty encouraging patients to exercise
- The need to accommodate different physical abilities

---

## Solution Requirements

The proposed solution should be:

### Simple and Accessible

The system should:

- Be easy for senior patients to use
- Consider patients' limited experience with smartphones and smart devices
- Require minimal technical knowledge

### Adaptable

The system should:

- Support different exercise difficulty levels
- Allow exercise intensity to be adjusted based on patient needs
- Prevent excessive fatigue while encouraging activity

### Track Progress

The solution should:

- Use accessible technology to monitor user progress
- Identify changes in activity levels or physical ability over time

---

## Challenge Goal

Develop a solution that helps hospitalized senior patients stay physically active while improving engagement and overall well-being.

Consider:

- How the solution will be used by elderly patients
- How technology can make exercise more accessible
- How patient progress can be monitored
- How the solution can be adapted to different physical abilities
