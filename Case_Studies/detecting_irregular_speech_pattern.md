# Detecting Irregular Speech Patterns

## Case Study

### Background

Studies have shown that delirium can cause a variety of speech and language abnormalities, including:

- Incoherent speech
- Disorganized thought processes
- Difficulty maintaining attention during conversations
- Slurred speech
- Repetitive phrases
- Fragmented sentences

These changes can make it more difficult for healthcare professionals to identify delirium quickly and accurately.

### Speech Changes Associated With Delirium

Speech irregularities in delirium are not only related to cognitive changes but may also reflect emotional changes.

Patients experiencing delirium may show:

- Increased fear or distress in their speech
- Sudden changes in speaking speed
- Changes in tone or emotional expression

Although these abnormalities can be subtle, they may provide important indicators of an underlying medical condition.

## Challenge

You are tasked with exploring ways to better understand and monitor speech changes associated with delirium.

Speech recognition technology and Natural Language Processing (NLP) tools have previously been used to study conditions such as:

- Mood disorders
- Cognitive decline

However, these technologies have not yet been extensively applied to detecting the onset of delirium.

Your goal is to explore how speech-based analysis could provide new insights into delirium detection.

---

## Research Goals

Drawing on interdisciplinary research, investigate the following areas:

### 1. Identify Speech Patterns

Explore:

- What speech characteristics are associated with delirium?
- Which language or acoustic features may indicate changes in cognition?

### 2. Analyze Speech Data

Investigate:

- How speech recognition and NLP technologies can measure these changes
- What computational methods could be used to analyze speech patterns
- What types of data would be required for such a system

### 3. Consider Clinical Impact

Evaluate:

- How earlier detection of delirium could improve patient outcomes
- How speech-based monitoring could support healthcare professionals

---

## Expected Outcome

You are **not required to develop a complete solution**.

Instead, your goal is to:

- Explore possible approaches for detecting delirium through speech data
- Discuss technical, ethical, and medical considerations
- Suggest potential next steps for future development
- Identify additional data sources that may be needed if existing datasets are unavailable
